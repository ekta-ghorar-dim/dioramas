# v16 review: "Low Frequency" (concept C07, Dioramas "Low Frequency" world ported)

**Concept:** "Rewind the moment." A dim violet-amber listening room with a playable turntable. Press play and an original loop is synthesised live in WebAudio; drag the record to scratch (playback rate follows your hand), the way a replay operator scrubs a moment. Audio-reactive speaker, light and dust.
- **Sections:** tonight's running order (A1 holding-screen bed T−15, A2 draft T−5, A3 game one 00:00, A4 replay and stats) as cueable tracks; "The gallery" house rules ("No dead air", positions, sound mix, sponsors on the sheet); 18 / 4 / 3 / 3; **the catalogue**, where record sleeves *and the 3D record wall* now show AOBS's real key visuals (PCC, Women's War, prize-pool reveal, Halloween Fest, Mini Mayhem, PCC registration); production models as "tiers"; the quote.
- The 3D record label is rebranded AOBS / REPLAY.
**Look:** violet night, amber; Syne + Space Mono.
