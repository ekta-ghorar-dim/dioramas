# v14 review: "Drifting Isles" (concept C12, Dioramas "Atlas" world ported)

**Concept:** "Four places on our map. One crew for all of them." A luminous sky of soft clouds with four floating islands, flocking birds and an airship. Click an island or scroll and the camera flies there. Each island is a place AOBS has broadcast, with a ticket-style panel of real facts:
- **Online:** AOBS Cloud remote production.
- **Bangladesh:** DIU Winter Fest teaser; Bangla sponsor copy on Mini Mayhem.
- **Pakistan:** the Pakistan Community Cup grand final, Bo5, 30,000-diamond prize pool, Potatos Gaming.
- **Nepal:** the MLBB Nepal teaser and Battle of Gurkha's.

"The fleet" becomes AOBS's three tools (AOBS Cloud = carrier, OBS Plugins = rigging, Backup Streamer = lifeboat). The booking form ("where + game + first match day") opens the real request form with answers pre-filled. A live "broadcast map" chart tracks the flight.
**Look:** sky blue, cream, coral; Young Serif + Nunito Sans. Storybook but premium.
