# v17 review: "Geode" (concept C10, Dioramas "Lithos" world ported)

**Concept:** "A raw stream is a stone." A rough, closed stone on velvet under a starfield. Press and hold: violet cracks glow along the seam, the stone trembles and splits into a sparkling amethyst cavity ("Cracked open: what a raw stream becomes when AOBS produces it"). The cursor is a light that orbits the specimen.
- **Four facets of one broadcast:** the In-game HUD (PCC grand final), the Draft Board (Bo5), the Sponsor Formats (Mini Mayhem × Shadhin Host, 3 placements, as pyrite cubes), the Second Chamber (Backup Streamer, as the chambered ammonite).

- **Provenance:** a real PCC frame plus three principles (real output named, data not typing, a second path always). Then 18 / 4 / 3 and the quote.
- The original miner photo (a person) was replaced with a real broadcast frame.
**Look:** velvet black, amethyst; Italiana + Cinzel + Jost.
