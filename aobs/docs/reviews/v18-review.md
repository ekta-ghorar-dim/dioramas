# v18 review: "Kōen" (concept C21, Dioramas "Kōen" world ported)

**Concept:** "One tournament, four seasons." A maple bonsai on washi-paper light. Scroll or the season dial turns its year; here the four seasons are the life of a league:
- **I Registration** (first leaves: key visual, registration, slots left, prize-pool reveal; the PCC campaign)
- **II Group stage** (deep green: the same match-day running order, remote production, two stream paths)
- **III The final** (the red week: draft board, HUD, stage, trophy; the PCC grand final)
- **IV Off-season** (bare branches: the structure, the next brief)

Then events on record (hover shows each identity), three tools as "the craft" (AOBS Cloud, OBS Plugins, Backup Streamer as the roots), and a season brief. Japanese seal and season glyphs are replaced with AOBS marks and Roman numerals; the season dial reads "season n".
**Look:** washi paper, ink, maple red; Cormorant Garamond.
